About Me

Builder.
Designer.
Engineer.

I'm Abd'samad — Balo for short. I'm a full-stack developer and UI/UX designer working out of Lagos, Nigeria. I build complete digital products from concept to deployment: the backend that holds things together, the interface people actually enjoy using, and everything in between.

I started in hardware at Computer Village, Ikeja, learning how machines work at the component level. Then I trained formally at Aptech International in software engineering and UI/UX design. That combination shapes how I think about software: I care about how things work all the way down, not just what they look like on the surface.
